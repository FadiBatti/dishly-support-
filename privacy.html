Dishly Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Dishly – Scan, Shop & Cook respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Process

Dishly may process ingredient lists, shopping lists, food preferences, dietary preferences, allergies, and other information you provide to use app features.

2. Camera and Image Processing

Dishly uses your device's camera when you choose to scan food products or ingredients.

Images may be transmitted securely to an external service for image analysis. According to our processing arrangements, images are deleted after processing and are not retained for ongoing storage.

Camera access requires your permission.

3. Advertising

Dishly uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, approximate location, advertising interaction data, and diagnostic information.

Advertising-related data practices depend on your device settings, applicable consent choices, and Google's policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Use

Information is processed to provide app features, analyze ingredients, display recipes, improve reliability, and support advertising.

5. Data Sharing

Data may be processed by third-party providers that support the application's features, including image analysis and advertising services.

6. Data Retention

Images submitted for analysis are deleted after processing, as described above. Other information may be retained according to the practices of relevant service providers and applicable law.

7. Your Choices

You may choose whether to grant camera permission and may manage applicable privacy and advertising preferences through the app or device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Dishly is not specifically directed to children. We do not intentionally request personal information from children for account registration.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page.
